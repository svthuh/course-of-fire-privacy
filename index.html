Course of Fire – Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Course of Fire ("the app") is a stage builder, shot timer and scoring tool for precision rifle, NRL22 / rimfire and pistol / carbine shooting. It is developed by Michael Leaf. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data.

Information the app collects

None. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and it does not send your information to the developer or to anyone else.

Microphone

The shot timer asks for microphone permission so it can hear your shots and record their times. While the timer or the microphone test is running, sound is analyzed on your device, in real time, only to detect loud sounds such as shots and the timer's own start beep.

  • Audio is not recorded, saved to a file or kept after it has been analyzed.
  • Audio is never sent off your device.
  • The microphone is used only while the timer or the microphone test is running. You can turn the permission off at any time in your phone's settings; the timer then works as a manual stopwatch.

Data stored on your device

The stages you build, your range setup, timer settings and the runs you score are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Sharing

The app shares information only when you choose to. When you use Share, Print, PDF, Picture, CSV or a Course of Fire (.cof) file, the app hands the content to your phone's share sheet or print service, and you pick where it goes, for example email or a messaging app. Anything you send that way is then handled under the privacy policy of the app or service you choose. When you import a file, the app reads only the file you pick.

Permissions

  • Microphone – shot detection, as described above.
  • Files you choose – only when you import or share a file.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
