The apps

Course of Fire is for building courses of fire for precision rifle, NRL22 / rimfire, and pistol / carbine, then running them on a shot timer and scoring the result. Michael Leaf makes the apps. They share Course of Fire files (.cof) when you choose to export and import them.

iPhone and iPad

Course of Fire, bundle ID com.michaelleaf.courseoffire. Stage library, generator, printable stage sheet, shot timer, and scoring. Support

Android

The same app, package com.michaelleaf.courseoffire. Support

Windows

The desktop stage builder. It has no microphone and no account. Stages are saved on that computer. You can export a .cof file and open it in the phone app, and the other way around.

Privacy policy

Effective November 3, 2026. This policy covers the iPhone app, the Android app, and the Windows app.

In short: these apps do not collect, store on a server, sell, or share your personal data.

Information the apps collect

None. There are no accounts and no sign-in. The apps contain no advertising, analytics, or tracking software. They do not send your stages, scores, or audio to the developer or to anyone else.

Microphone

On iPhone and Android, the shot timer asks for microphone permission so it can hear shots and record their times. While the timer or the microphone test is running, sound is analyzed on the device, in real time, only to detect loud sounds such as shots and the timer's own start beep.

  • Audio is not recorded, saved to a file, or kept after it has been analyzed.
  • Audio is never sent off the device.
  • The microphone is used only while the timer or the microphone test is running. You can turn the permission off in the phone's settings. The timer then works as a stopwatch you stop yourself.

The Windows app does not use a microphone.

Data stored on your device

On iPhone and Android, the stages you build, your range setup, timer settings, and the runs you score are saved only in the app's private storage. Each save also keeps the previous copy there, so a damaged file can be recovered on the device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes that storage.

On Windows, stages are saved in the app's private folder for your Windows user account. They stay on that computer.

Clipboard

If you copy a stage as text on the phone, the app places that text on the clipboard. It does not read anything else from the clipboard, and it does not send the clipboard anywhere.

Sharing and files

The apps share information only when you choose to. Share, print, PDF, picture, CSV, and Course of Fire (.cof) export hand the content to the share sheet, the print service, or a file you save. You pick where it goes. Anything you then send by email or another app is handled under that app's privacy policy.

When you import a file, the app reads only the file you pick.

Permissions

  • Microphone, iPhone and Android only, for shot detection as described above.
  • Files you choose, only when you import or export a file.

Support messages

If you write through the iPhone or Android support form, that message is sent so it can be answered. The apps themselves do not send it. The form asks for your name, email, and message, and on iPhone it can include the phone and iOS version you type.

Children

The apps are not directed at children, and they do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
